Frequently asked questions

How much are eBay seller fees in 2026?
For most categories eBay charges a final value fee of about 13.6% of the total order (item + shipping + tax), plus a per-order fee of $0.30 on orders of $10 or less or $0.40 on orders over $10. The percentage ranges roughly 12% to 15% depending on the category.
Does eBay charge fees on shipping?
Yes. The final value fee is calculated on the full amount the buyer pays, including the shipping you charge and sales tax, not just the item price. This calculator applies the fee to item + shipping.
What is the eBay per-order fee?
On top of the percentage fee, eBay adds a flat fee per order: $0.30 if the order total is $10 or less, or $0.40 if it is over $10. It is charged once per order, not per item.
Why do eBay fees vary so much?
The final value percentage is set per category (roughly 12% to 15%). A paid Store subscription lowers it in many categories, while an account rated "Below Standard" pays an extra 6%. Set the rate field to your own category for an accurate result.
Is this calculator free?
Yes. It is free, needs no account, and stores nothing. Every calculation runs in your browser, so your numbers never leave your device.
