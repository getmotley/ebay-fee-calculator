Frequently asked questions

How much are eBay seller fees in 2026?
For most categories, about 13.6% of the total order (item + shipping + tax), plus a per-order fee of $0.30 on orders of $10 or less or $0.40 over $10. The percentage ranges roughly 12 to 15% by category.
Does eBay charge fees on shipping?
Yes. The final value fee is calculated on the full amount the buyer pays, including shipping and sales tax, not just the item price. This tool applies it to item + shipping.
What is the eBay per-order fee?
A flat fee per order on top of the percentage: $0.30 if the order total is $10 or less, or $0.40 if it is over $10. Charged once per order, not per item.
Why do eBay fees vary so much?
The final value percentage is set per category (roughly 12 to 15%). A paid Store subscription lowers it, while a "Below Standard" account pays an extra 6%. Set the rate above to your own category.
